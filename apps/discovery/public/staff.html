Proposed topics

Accept / remap / reject / merge the topics the LLM proposed against the closed controlled vocabulary. Locked until the enrichment run completes, so the vocabulary doesn’t shift mid-pass.

  • proposed_topic escape hatch surfaced here — e.g. “Strikes” wants promoting under Economy & Labor.
  • Accept → adds to vocabulary · Remap → folds into an existing topic · Reject → drops.
  • Mirrors CN’s Facet review stage — a dedicated pass downstream of extraction.
Budget goes to Review + Shape review first

Entities & merge

Dedup people / places / orgs — and the print-native additions: venue · performer · creative_work. Auto-suggested merges by substring, abbreviation, and prefix; place-hierarchy assignment.

  • “A.F. of L.” · “A. F. of L.” · “American Federation of Labor” → one entity.
  • Place hierarchy: Corrigan-McKinney → Cleveland → Cuyahoga County → Ohio.

Low-confidence queue

The escape hatch for reads no first pass could trust — low transcription quality or suspected hallucination. Re-OCR at higher fidelity, route to the two-stage segment→crop→VLM fallback, or hand to human transcription.

  • Dense multi-column pages (the Scene stress case) land here when reading-order confidence drops.
  • Excluded from full-text search until resolved — never index a read the pipeline itself distrusts.
  • Analog of CN’s low-OCR / illegible worklist — same “needs another look” discipline.

Illustration review

Confirm / relabel extracted images and their types — photo · cartoon · map · chart · ad-cut. Transcribe the printed caption (the reliable signal); the crop is served visually via IIIF, not described by the machine.

  • Text-first, scoped: no generated visual description in the prototype phase.
  • degraded_halftone flag for poor microfilm reproduction.
  • Confirmed illustrations carry into discovery as first-class objects (the “Pictures & Ads” visual wall).
